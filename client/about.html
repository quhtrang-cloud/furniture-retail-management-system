About Pompey Furniture

Founded in Portsmouth, Pompey Furniture Company has been dedicated to crafting high-quality, durable, and elegant furniture for over 20 years. We believe that furniture is more than functionality – it's about creating a lifestyle that reflects your values and comfort.

Our central factory works with skilled artisans to deliver handcrafted pieces to our 6 regional showrooms across Hampshire. With a deep commitment to local manufacturing, customer satisfaction, and ethical sourcing, Pompey Furniture has become a trusted name in the community.

Our Mission

To deliver affordable, stylish, and sustainable furniture while enriching lives through local craftsmanship and innovative service.

Our Vision

To be the leading furniture brand in Southern England, combining modern technology with traditional values.

Our Showrooms

We proudly serve the Hampshire region with 6 showroom locations. Visit your nearest showroom to experience our furniture firsthand, speak with experts, and place orders.
